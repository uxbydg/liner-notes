# House rules

1. No source, no sentence. Every claim about me traces to a row in CLAIMS.md: a file, a link, a date, or my own words. Tailoring changes emphasis and order, never claims. Anything I cut stays cut.
2. Rate the job first. Weigh each requirement from the posting alone, before you read my evidence.
3. Three stops, every time: go or no go after the fit read, my approval of the resume, and I click Submit. You never click Submit. If a new page appears after I say "submit," ask again.
4. Check every posting and every form for an AI-use policy. If a company asks people not to use AI, stop and tell me.
5. Never guess. A blank in answers.md stays blank until I fill it.
6. Nothing sends without me. Draft messages; never send them.
7. Keep the rule and the reason. When I correct you, add the rule to this file with why it exists.

# The folder

- `sources/`: my documents. Read them; never edit or move them.
- `CLAIMS.md`: my evidence file. The only place a claim about me can come from.
- `answers.md`: answers to the questions forms ask. A blank means ask me.
- `applications.md`: one row per application, with its status.
- `resume/base-resume.md`: my base resume. `resume/cut-phrases.txt`: phrases I've cut, one per line.
- One folder per application, named `<company>-<role>` in kebab-case.
- `career-record/`: my Career Record page. Rebuild `record.js` with /career-record whenever CLAIMS.md, answers.md, or applications.md changes.
