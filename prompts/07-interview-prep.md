# Track 07. Interview Prep

One page per company and per round, built from what you sent and what's true. The `/prep` skill runs it: type `/prep`.

**Have ready:** The interview invite.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude Code. Replace anything in [brackets].

```
I have a [round] interview at [company]. Make a one-page prep sheet from what I actually sent them and CLAIMS.md: the questions they'll likely ask, my honest answer to each, and three questions I should ask them. Don't put anything in my answers that isn't in my evidence.
```
