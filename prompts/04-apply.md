# Track 04. Apply to One Role

A fit read, a tailored resume, and the form filled in, with three stops. The `/apply` skill runs the whole thing: type `/apply <link>`.

**Have ready:** One posting link.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude Code. Replace anything in [brackets].

```
Here's a job posting: [link]. Check it for an AI-use policy first. Then weigh each requirement from the posting alone, before you read CLAIMS.md. Tell me: apply or skip, my two biggest gaps, and the interview question each gap will trigger. Then stop and wait for my go.
```
