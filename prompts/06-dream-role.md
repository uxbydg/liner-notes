# Track 06. The Dream Role (bonus track)

Make something small for that one team, then ask one person for their input on it. Never for a call.

**Have ready:** A role you'd really want, and an evening.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude Code. Replace anything in [brackets].

```
I'd love to work at [company]. Research their product and the team I'd join, and find one problem I could actually help with. Help me make one small thing about it in an evening. Then draft a short note to one person there asking for their input on it. Don't ask for a call, and don't send anything.
```
