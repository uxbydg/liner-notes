# Track 01. Your Evidence File

Everything you've actually done, each line with its proof. It reads your documents first, shows you where they disagree, then asks about the rest. Everything after this can only use what's here.

**Have ready:** Your documents in `sources/`: your resume, LinkedIn as a PDF, offer letters, old job descriptions, certificates, and reviews.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude Code. Replace anything in [brackets].

```
Build my evidence file. Read every document in my sources folder, and show me where they disagree before you ask anything else. Then make CLAIMS.md: one row per claim I make, with its proof next to it (a file, a link, a date, or my own words). A job description shows what the role was, not what I did, so ask me before you claim anything from one. If a claim has no proof, don't guess. Ask me about it, one question at a time, and cut anything I can't back up.
```
